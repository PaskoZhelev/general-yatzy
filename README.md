# General Yatzy - Dice Game

Url: https://PaskoZhelev.github.io/general-yatzy/

# Rules:

 ## 1. Upper Section & Bonus


Categories: Standard 1s through 6s (sum of specific dice).

The Bonus: Achieving a total of 63 points or higher in the Upper Section grants a +50 point bonus.

## 2. The "General" (5-of-a-Kind)


Variable Scoring: Unlike standard Yatzy (50 pts), a General is worth (Die Value × 10) + 40.

1s=50, 2s=60, 3s=70, 4s=80, 5s=90, 6s=100.

General Joker: A second General can be used to fill an empty field in the Small or Large Straight categories.

## 3. The Lower Section (Combinations)


One Pair (2): Sum of the 2 matching dice.

Two Pair (2+2): Sum of all 4 dice in the pairs.

Three of a Kind (3): Sum of the 3 matching dice.

Four of a Kind (4): Sum of the 4 matching dice.

Full House (3+2): Sum of all 5 dice.

Even: Requirement: All 5 dice must be even (2, 4, or 6). Score: Sum of all dice (Max 30).

Odd: Requirement: All 5 dice must be odd (1, 3, or 5). Score: Sum of all dice (Max 25).

## 4. The Straights (Specific Sequences)


Small Straight: Must be exactly 1-2-3-4-5. Fixed 30 points.

Large Straight: Must be exactly 2-3-4-5-6. Fixed 40 points.

## 5. The Dual Chance Rule


Small Chance: Sum of all 5 dice.

Large Chance: Sum of all 5 dice.

Constraint: The value recorded in Large Chance must be greater or equal to the Small Chance. If a roll is lower than the value in Small Chance, the player must "0" the Large Chance or place the score elsewhere.
